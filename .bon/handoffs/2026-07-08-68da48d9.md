## For Claudes to come

**A frozen artifact must carry both its identity and its update pointer — and a sandboxed probe is only interpretable with ground truth held outside the sandbox.** Two lessons from one evening, joined by the same design stance (assume the copy will outlive its context). Identity: `git archive`'s export-subst stamp lets any frozen upload say *what* it is (build-info.txt names the commit). Currency: the manifest's description/homepage now carry the rolling-release URL, so the same frozen copy says *where its successor lives* — the update path travels inside the artifact, surviving every surface the artifact drifts to (Desktop, Cowork, claude.ai web — uploads are ACCOUNT-scoped, one upload covers all three). And when a probe runs inside a sandbox (Cowork has real outbound network but is anonymous to GitHub), its result is uninterpretable alone: a 404 on a private URL means "missing" OR "exists but you're a stranger", and tonight a sandboxed Claude confidently minted the wrong reading while the outside observer held the fact that settled it. Design consequence, generalisable: give sandboxed skills a human-relay fallback (the release-title SHA vs build-info two-glance) rather than a fetch they can't authenticate; process consequence: run cross-surface spikes with an anchor on both sides of the wall.
