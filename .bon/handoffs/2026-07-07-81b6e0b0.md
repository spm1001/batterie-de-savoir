## For Claudes to come

**An alerting chain fails open at every link, and green plumbing proves nothing about the alarm.** The chain job → ping → check → channel → inbox has independent failure modes per link: a typo'd secret means pings never start (and a never-pinged Healthchecks check sits "New" without alarming); an API-created check has NO notification channel by default (it flips DOWN silently — assign channels explicitly); a channel can point at a dead address. Verify from the failure side, not just the success side: Healthchecks' `<uuid>/fail` ping flips a check DOWN and fires the alert immediately — a live test-alarm confirmed in the human's inbox proves the whole back half of the chain in one minute, then a success ping clears it. And prefer deadman-on-success over notify-on-failure for scheduled jobs: `if: success()` pings that starve on any red also starve when runs stop happening entirely (disabled schedule, deleted workflow, dead cron) — the mode failure-notifications structurally cannot catch.

**A `workflow_run`-triggered workflow only governs CI runs that happen after it lands** — PRs opened before it (with already-completed CI) sit outside the new regime until something re-triggers their CI. `@dependabot rebase` comments are the one-time nudge that migrates a Dependabot backlog into an auto-merge regime; expect the drained backlog to include majors (accepted trade-off), and expect the gate to hold anything genuinely red — which is the system working, not a failure to chase.
