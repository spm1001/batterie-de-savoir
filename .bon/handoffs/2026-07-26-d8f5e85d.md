## For Claudes to come

**`scripts/` IS vendored, and that decides whether a change needs a suite bump.** `assemble.sh` vendors `commands skills agents hooks scripts .mcp.json CLAUDE.md instructions.md`. So a `scripts/lint.py` or `scripts/batterie-lint.py` edit rides a suite bump and will be quarantined by the ratchet without one — but `brigade.toml`, `docs/` and `.bon/` are source-only and free. Check which side of that line a file sits on *before* planning the publish, not after.

**The verification lesson that nearly cost a false report.** I patched a plugin cache copy so a fresh session could prove the fix, then a third session updated plugins and moved the load path from `1.21.3` to `1.21.4`. My patch was still on disk, in a directory nothing loaded. Had I not re-checked, I would have said "restart and verify" and the restart would have reproduced the original error, making a correct fix look broken. **Re-derive which file is live at the moment you verify, not at the moment you plan.**
