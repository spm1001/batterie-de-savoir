The session lifecycle architecture is being redesigned around a single primary artifact: the handoff. Currently /close produces six outputs (handoff, contribution, staged garde extraction, bon updates, git commit, and triggers garde ingest). The new design: /close writes one handoff with two zones — a Now zone (Gotchas/Risks/Next/Commands) consumed by the next /open for orientation, and a Compost zone (Done/Reflection/Learned) processed overnight into understanding.md updates and garde extractions. The Learned section replaces the separate contribution file but serves the same purpose — one paragraph of architectural knowledge that /open actively synthesizes into understanding.md as an onboarding act. Overnight composting is a new daily process that reads unprocessed handoffs across all repos, produces garde extractions by parsing handoff sections (not an LLM call), and falls back to cold JSONL processing via deglacer only for sessions without handoffs. MEMORY.md is explicitly Anthropic's domain — the rich handoff content in session transcripts feeds autoDream naturally without us writing to ~/.claude/ directly. The auto-handoff (session-end hook for unclosed sessions) drops its background Opus call and becomes a thin mechanical safety net (git + bon state, instant, no race condition). Scratch repos route handoffs to target repos based on bon prefixes rather than accumulating knowledge locally. This spans bon (handoff format, /close, /open), garde (handoff-native adapter, retire staged extraction), and a new overnight composting process.
