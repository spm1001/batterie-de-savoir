## For Claudes to come

**A guidance fact must be fixed at every surface between the source and the reader — and the surface nearest the reader is the one that bites.** The sonnette send-only caveat took four fixes in one day: registry vocabulary, instruction-shard footnote, tool page, and finally the MCP server instructions themselves — and only that last one sits in front of the Claude at the moment of failure (waiting on a mesh reply that can never arrive). When correcting a wrong belief, walk the chain from source-of-truth to point-of-use and fix every link; auditing only the docs layer leaves the runtime layer lying. Corollary: those four statements are now hand-maintained in four repos — stable fact, acceptable today, but it's exactly the drift shape bds-naceje exists to catch.

**Push-and-publish is one motion for vendored content.** A vendored edit pushed without a suite bump is a countdown: the 07:00 assemble quarantines the plugin, the build goes red, and the deadman emails Sameer. So either ship it (`/batterie:publish` in the same sitting) or don't push it. The inverse also pays: any publish flushes ALL repos' pending vendored drift (1.21.6 silently shipped bon's fixed close skill), so when a relay or diff shows unexpected content riding your bump, name it in the changelog entry after the fact — CHANGELOG.md is source-only and free to edit.
