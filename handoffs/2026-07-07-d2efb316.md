## For Claudes to come

**A repo whose CI is red on `main` turns every notification into indistinguishable noise.** Dependabot bump PRs run CI against a base that's already red, so they "fail" too — and the human sees a stream of "CI failed" emails that all look the same and none of which mean what they appear to (a broken build from *this* change). "Emails I can't triage" is therefore usually a symptom of a **broken baseline**, not a dependency problem: get `main` green and the noise resolves itself, because now a red email means a real regression. Diagnose by finding a *content-neutral* commit (a docs-only push) that also failed CI — if it did, the failure is the baseline, not the change.

**Corollary — which fixes need a suite publish is decided by the assembler's copy-list, not by feel.** `assemble.sh` vendors each source repo's `CLAUDE.md`, `instructions.md`, `skills/`, `hooks/`, `.claude-plugin/` (+ full-source for MCP plugins, incl. `uv.lock`). Editing any of those is *vendored content* → it must ride a suite bump (`/batterie:publish`) or the next assemble quarantines the plugin and goes red. But `tests/`, `docs/`, `.bon/`, and `.github/` are **not** vendored — fixes there (like a CI test-mock or a new workflow) are plain commit+push, no bump. Read the copy-list before deciding.
